## Interaction contracts

- Pass is available at the start of a human turn alongside the other top-level actions. Choosing a piece or preview does not start an action. An accepted action does. Ending a legal continuation is End turn for humans, computers and training. Required supply, retreat and follow obligations remain enforced. End turn has durable evidence but no move row or move number.
- On a visible live board, a human's acknowledged push follow is automatic only when Follow is the sole legal action. Multiple follows, a legal End turn/stop alternative, computer-controlled turns, history and recovery stay outside this automation. Reconcile durable intent before admission, deduplicate the authoritative state, and keep browser-tab admission under one shared lock where supported. The existing journal exclusively owns admitted commands and retries. Hold the after-retreat board until acknowledgement, then animate the recorded follow and sound it once before considering another mandatory follow. Navigation and backgrounding cancel visuals without canceling durable delivery. Reduced motion shows the acknowledged follow statically.
- Show me again for a local push has a separate action and replays exactly its acknowledged push, defender retreat and all follows, including when the sequence begins midway through a turn. It never replays unrelated turn actions or writes gameplay. Keep this source available while the next player or computer is waiting/thinking. A new local action retires it, and the following opponent-turn narration keeps its own full-turn replay source. History, piece inspection, navigation, account changes and new revisions retain the existing replay cancellation rules.
- The commander and supply legend controls inspect the selected piece, or the side to move when nothing is selected. An unoccupied supply point inspects all paths to that point. Pieces and legal destinations take priority. Inspection works while computer actions run. Commander reclicks cycle through the agreed piece and network views.
- Board messages describe the current task briefly. Routine computer phases appear only while the human waits, following the [message contract](../RIGHELT_WEB_APP_SPEC.md#stable-board-messages-and-inline-actions). Preparation says Waiting for the named computer. Confirmed completed-action narration replaces routine activity. Show me again visually replays the acknowledged computer turn over an isolated board. It never submits, searches, selects history or mutates the live game. Stop, navigation, backgrounding, account changes, undo and new live revisions cancel playback. Reduced motion uses static manual steps.
- Thinking copy has no literal ellipsis. The animated loading pieces supply the cue and keep their identity across preparation, search and save. Recovery, history, replay and paused states stop the cue.
- Play again and Create a new game here use mutually exclusive native opponent mini cards, with the current opponent selected. Portraits, cut corners, keyboard arrows, visible focus and 44-pixel labels follow the existing design. Readiness updates preserve selected controls and focus. Start stays disabled while the source game is recovering, and actual start errors persist through background updates.
- Thinking, saving and related activity animations use the responsible player’s red or blue. Pending work keeps its captured actor after optimistic turn advancement, including defender-controlled retreats. Color updates preserve node identity and animation time. Unknown-actor page loading retains the dual-color brand treatment.
- A valid unresolved receipt remains pending with its original durable command, retry ownership and deadline. Actionable authentication, storage and integrity failures appear immediately. Routine transport failures follow the active-visible-wait grace in [recovery](GAME_RECOVERY.md#presentation-and-diagnostics). Recovery offers icon-labeled Reload game and Home actions without discarding pending intent.
- Ordinary thinking exposes no recovery CTA. Preparation detail starts after 5 seconds, Interrupt after 30 seconds, and save feedback after 10 seconds in the save phase. Actionable authentication, storage and integrity failures appear immediately. Routine transport failures retain the active-visible-wait grace above. Slow feedback lives outside the board card.
- Connection recovery notices count only visible time waiting on blocked live play in the active game. Show a quiet notice after 4 seconds and stronger recovery actions after 15 seconds. Returning from a hidden tab, changing games or accounts, entering history, and settled recovery reset this presentation wait. An overdue background command cannot bypass the fresh grace. Durable command deadlines, lease authority and retry timing remain wall-clock based. Authentication, update, invalid-response and local-storage failures remain immediately actionable. Recovery notices stay separate from board task messages.
- Before a gameplay command is admitted, computer play automatically retries temporary resource/evidence request failures, HTTP 408/429/5xx, incomplete transfers and request timeouts. Client backoff caps at 5 seconds, while a longer server Retry-After is honored. A completed evidence write with a lost response is read back by the same attempt ID and compared to its exact JSON payload before an identical write or submission. It must never be replaced with unfinished evidence. A completed decision survives temporary admission fences without another calculation, attempt ID or evidence rewrite while its scope, revision, fingerprint and control generation remain current. Retries recheck game, account, transport, revision, state fingerprint, control generation and live acknowledged lease. Scoped cancellations discard the old unadmitted decision. After gameplay admission, only the durable journal owns retries and receipt reconciliation. Integrity, protocol, ownership, authentication and storage failures remain explicit recovery states. Pre-admission retry notices reuse the foreground 4/15-second clock outside the board, with Retry available after the stronger threshold.

## Space and history

- Board heading and routine prompt geometry stay stable through selection and waiting. The approved [message contract](../RIGHELT_WEB_APP_SPEC.md#stable-board-messages-and-inline-actions) reserves finite routine grammar. Opponent summaries, replay narration and extended explanations use independently scrollable Board context outside that prompt, without growing Board height. Hidden sizing replicas use matching primary/fallback fonts and text scale. Loading uses the same shell and board geometry as the settled page. Home uses the same card and preview structure before data arrives. Existing content stays mounted through refresh and pagination. A socket snapshot arriving before route hydration uses the same canonical game shell and authority gates, with no intermediate legacy board frame.
- Narrow layout has one document scroller. Inactive panels do not contribute height. The header uses one row, with Account in the menu. Three lightweight icons provide Players, Board and History. Navigation hides after 3 seconds idle or 24 pixels down, and returns after 12 pixels up, panel entry or its accessible handle. Keyboard interaction, safe areas, scroll restoration and overscroll must not cause accidental hiding or interception.
- Narrow Board always projects the live game. History uses compact owner rails and Turn N headings, with names retained in accessible attribution. All displayed move rows are newest first, including within a turn. Replay chronology remains unchanged. Selecting a move or turn shows the same overlays in desktop and mobile. Individual selected entries are inert while their child actions remain interactive. Turn summaries have no undo/new-game actions. See [History presentation](HISTORY_PRESENTATION.md) for source/target colors, aggregate routes, frozen selections and mobile reveal. Players preserves selection. Any return to Board clears history before revealing it. Return to live also switches to Board. Resizing carries the selected view between layouts.
- Accepted undo returns only its requester to live, provided they have not changed history selection since initiating it. Browsing away and back still counts as a change. Inspection and visual replay do not. Pending undo remains a notice so browsing remains possible. Other viewers retain their own selections. Archived decisions use authoritative restoration identity.

## Motion and presentation

- Sound for a newly received opponent move follows its authoritative move ID across sockets, snapshots and reconciliation. Initial history and older history expansion are silent. Live opponent moves still notify while the person inspects history or replay. Computer optimistic commands do not receive human-local feedback or suppress their later authoritative cue. Human placement and its acknowledgement remain deduplicated. Foreground batched catch-up gives each move a short separate cue; mute, leaving and backgrounding cancel queued foreground cues. Background play retains one initial incoming cue and one 30-second reminder loop until the active live Board is visible and pending playback has completed. Focus or navigation to Players or History does not acknowledge unseen moves. Mute pauses reminders without clearing unseen state.

- Notifications retain incident identity, focus, open details and animation time during updates. Active work pulses its whole surface every 2.4 seconds. Waiting for a human uses a slower 4-second pulse. Copy and controls stay steady. Errors, completion and paused states are static. A new urgent notice must not hide a focused or expanded notice.
- Loading sweeps have crisp red and blue facets, smooth transforms and no blended edge or blur. An independent reviewer inspects the sweep, pulses, replay and narrow layout together for coherence with Righelt's stone-cut design.
- Computer home cards name the opponent rather than saying both players. User-facing experimental copy and redundant UI instructions are removed. First-use explanatory tooltips are deferred.
