## Readable physical hierarchy

Use depth to explain role. Main sections carry content. Cards are lighter objects. Board cells and editable fields receive pieces or text. Flyouts are a different object laid above the table. Supporting rows, facts, History entries and notices do not need their own carved containers.

Material belongs to the component. Do not place one decorative bitmap over an entire interactive app, apply a filter to foreground text or rebuild procedural grain for every cell/render. Static bounded textures should load independently of content geometry, retain an editable source and fall back to a readable surface. Inspect repetition, scroll, zoom, font fallback and cold loading at normal size.

Keep texture quiet beneath body copy, form values, diagnostics and game paths. Stronger seams belong mainly to outer margins and exposed cuts. A material change must not obscure the difference between read-only data and editable fields, or make a decorative red vein look like an error or move target.

Artwork must continue to integrate with its surrounding face through clean transparency or an exact surface match. Adapt illustrated game props, including tables, boards and pieces, to marble. Preserve approved characters, environmental settings, composition, narrative copy, carousel/readiness behavior and actual game vocabulary, except the explicit Friend modal composition below. Do not introduce halos, boxed illustrations or shrink scenes into thumbnails to conceal a material seam. An unrelated illustration rewrite is not implied.
