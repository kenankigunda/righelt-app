# Commander defeat and result feedback

A commander removed by a push must be reported as destroyed. An existing commander without supply keeps the supply-loss reason. The engine and browser runtime share the outcome classifier. Existing persisted games are interpreted from their terminal pieces when their old reason is ambiguous, without changing any saved game, move, receipt, model or training data.

Every decisive result names the winner, such as “Player 1 wins”. The player's established side determines victory or defeat feedback. Spectators and computer-versus-computer games remain neutral. Self-play keeps the selected starting-side perspective. Draws remain neutral outcomes for both sides.

The commander emblem and cut stones use the existing player colors. An observed live victory has one finite rising-and-settling effect. Defeat has a quieter settling effect. Reduced motion keeps the static emblem. Repaints retain elapsed animation time. Initial hydration, reload, history and manually opening a result never start an effect. Navigation, backgrounding and account/storage changes cancel it. Sound uses existing mute, focus and visibility preferences, with distinct brief rising and soft falling cues only after confirmed live completion.

Validation covers actual no-retreat commander removal through its legal continuation, genuine loss of supply, mixed draw causes, generated-runtime parity, saved legacy results, perspective, finite animation ownership and confirmed sound. Real result workflows verify history/reload/manual opening and reachable actions at narrow and landscape sizes. Final integration must retain the native opponent cards from the preceding board UX release.

All production games and complete move history are permanent training data, including synthetic smoke games. This work authorizes no hosted deletion, retention cleanup, reset, account activation or destructive migration. Local process cleanup is permitted. Evidence publication and schedules stay paused. Merge follows the ordered UX releases and requires the lead's coordinated slot.
